GeoOrbit Lab

Subsurface energy, seismic interpretation, and artificial intelligence.

GeoOrbit Lab integrates expertise in subsurface energy systems, seismic interpretation, reservoir characterization, geological modeling, and geoscience software development to address complex challenges in the analysis and interpretation of Earth systems. Our research focuses on the integration of machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and statistical inference with seismic attributes, seismic inversion, rock physics, geological and reservoir modeling, and field based sensing and robotics. Particular emphasis is placed on developing methods capable of identifying geological patterns, characterizing spatial heterogeneity, predicting subsurface properties, and quantifying uncertainty across multiple spatial and temporal scales. These approaches combine data driven learning with geological and physical constraints to improve model robustness, interpretability, and transferability to complex subsurface environments. The resulting workflows support a broad range of applications, including hydrocarbon and sustainable energy production, carbon capture and geological sequestration, geothermal resource development, subsurface monitoring, reservoir management, and exploration in geologically complex or data limited environments.
